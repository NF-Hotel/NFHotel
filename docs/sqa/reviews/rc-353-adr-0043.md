## Findings

- **The deploy of the VM warns; it does not stop.** The deliverable of [MIL-083] says that the deploy of either host stops on a `runc` below the floor. Decision 5 makes it a warning in the deploy of the VM, for the reason of the `--deploy` rule of MIL-080: the release cannot change the runtime, and a release with the floor that is rolled back for it would fail its rollback too, so S03 would have to restore by hand while nothing in the release is wrong. The timer's check fails, and its alert fires, every 15 minutes instead. The deploy of the monitoring stops as planned, and criterion 5 of the milestone (the tests) is unchanged. This is a stand-in decision; the gate review of MIL-083 records it, and S01 can challenge it in the summary of the run.
- **The node's requirement rests on reports of users.** No advisory of Proxmox names it. Task 2 reads `pveversion` before task 3, and a stack that does not start after the switch stops the work, with the snapshot as the way back; decision 4 rules out a weaker AppArmor profile as a way around it.
- **The floor is a list kept by hand, now with a test.** A person still reads the advisories (the first negative consequence). The first case of the page Container-Escape, `runc` 1.5.3, fixes an escape through checkpoint and restore, which NF Hotel does not use, so it does not enter the floor.

## Overall Verdict

Go. Every Mandatory criterion of QC-ADR-001 passes. ADR-0043 is Accepted as the AI's stand-in decision for S01 in autonomous mode, under the plan that S01 accepted in chat on 2026-11-14.
