## Findings

- **The gate waits for S01.** Only S01 can open the account at a mail relay and set the records of the domain. Everything else is built, tested and merged with the setting off; criterion 10 and the gate stay open until then.
- **Until the relay is set, the hole is narrowed and not closed.** With the setting off a public booking takes a room at once, as today, behind the limits of MIL-087. One address can no longer book the hotel in an hour; many addresses still can. That has to be said to S01 plainly.
- **A guest can lose the room between asking and verifying.** The page says so. The other way, a room held for a limited time, was rejected: whoever can hold a room without standing behind it can hold them all.
- **The word is verify.** Staff confirm a booking today, and the status Confirmed means that. The plan uses verify for the guest's step, so that one word does not mean two things.
- **A mail can land among the junk.** The records of the sender in task 9 are what helps, and the page offers to send the mail again.
- **The form must not become a way to mail strangers.** The mail holds nothing that the sender typed, one email has one open request, and the mails per email and per day are limited.
- **New moving parts.** The first timed job of the application and its first connection out to another party. ADR-0053 has to say how the API reaches the relay from its network, and that it reaches nothing else.
- **A new package for mail** comes into the supply chain; criterion 9 asks that it is pinned, locked and free of known vulnerabilities.
- **Mode.** Autonomous, as in RC-410.
