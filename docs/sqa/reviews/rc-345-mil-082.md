## Findings

- **Ollama is the root container that can change without a new design.** nginx needs its master as root for ports 80 and 443 until the image and the ports change ([CIS-001] row 4.1, Deferred), PostgreSQL drops to its own user after its start, and the socket proxy holds the Docker API, which is root on the host whatever user asks it, so its protection is who reaches it (MIL-081). Ollama runs as root only because its image does, and the benchmark has the pattern that gives a volume to a user.
- **The volume of production was written by root.** The CI test must start from such a volume, or it proves only a fresh install.
- **User namespaces stay a decision, not an omission.** The page evaluates them again with what they would cost here (the owners of every volume, PostgreSQL's data included) and what would change the decision.
