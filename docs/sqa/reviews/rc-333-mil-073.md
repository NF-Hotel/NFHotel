## Findings

- **Rule 1 of ADR-0040 now decides the remedy.** The build of 2026-11-12 lists `krb5-libs` 1.22.3-r0 in its SBOM attestation (read 2026-11-14), so the patch layer of rule 2 that the first version planned, with its twenty files and a restart of the database at every deploy, is not needed. The plan shrank from eight tasks to four.
- **The SBOM is not the scan.** A scan with Trivy on the PC failed twice on the download of its vulnerability database (a timeout from one mirror, a stream error from the other), so criterion 1 is decided by `scan-image.sh` in task 1 and by the checks of the pull request, not by the SBOM read.
- **The data is checked, not assumed.** 16.15 to 16.16 is a minor release, which keeps the data files; criterion 3 compares the row counts of every table before and after the deploy, and the deploy makes its backup first.
- **The entries of `gosu` stay as they are.** They expire on 2026-12-08 and gate only the daily scan, because the image stays a third-party image (finding 3); the first scan of the new build shows whether they still match.
- **Not independent.** The AI that walked the checklists wrote the plan; the acceptance is S01's decision.
