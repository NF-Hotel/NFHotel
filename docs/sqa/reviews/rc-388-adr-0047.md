## Findings

- **F1, fixed: who accepted the milestone.** The context said that S01 accepted [MIL-018] on 2026-11-14 and that S03
  approves the token, the cookie and the deploy changes. The AI accepted the revised plan as stand-in for S01, at
  S01's word in chat ([RC-358]), and S03 is held by S01 (the Stakeholder Analysis). Fixed in the text, and [RC-358]
  added to the CrossReference and the links.
- **F2, fixed: the reviewer of the new rows.** The new rows of ADR-0047 and of the other documents of MIL-018 named
  the AI as stand-in for S04. The approving reviewer of [MIL-018] is S01, and S01 gave the acceptance to the AI, so
  the rows say "AI as stand-in for S01".
- **F3, note: decision 7 refuses more than the milestone asked.** [MIL-018] asked that a sign-in be refused for a
  booking cancelled more than 30 days ago. The time of a cancellation is not stored and the milestone leaves the
  database unchanged, so every cancelled booking is refused; the ADR names it as a negative consequence, and [UC-010]
  keeps it as an open issue. Accepted by the AI as stand-in for S01: the stricter rule protects the guest data and
  adds no schema change. A guest who wants to see a cancelled booking asks the hotel.
- **F4, note: the receipt endpoint stays.** `POST /api/bookings/public/receipt` of [MIL-079] stays in the API with
  its rules and its throttle, although the web application no longer calls it. It answers only with the number and
  the email of the booking, as the guest sign-in does. Whether to remove it is S01's decision (action item).
- **F5, note: a stale number in the Business Case.** The scope of [BC-001] names ADR-0007 for this decision, a number
  that went to continuous integration and delivery. The ADR says so. The revision of the Business Case waits for
  S01's acceptance on its own branch, so the number is left there (action item).
- **F6, note: the weak secret and the token that cannot be revoked.** Whoever knows the number and the email of a
  booking can change it for two hours, and a copied token stays valid until its end. Both are named negatives, of
  the same kind as the throttle in memory of the staff sign-in ([ADR-0005]), and risks that S01 accepts with the ADR.
