## Findings

- **The numbers of decision 2 are defaults.** They come from the limits that the columns have today and from common practice; ADR-0050 may change one with its reason after the data that exists has been read.
- **Thirty nights were not asked for.** S01 asked for five years. Five years alone would still let one booking take a room for five years, and S01 also asked that nobody can book everything; staff can book a longer stay.
- **A rule that refuses a real name is a defect.** The tests of task 3 need names in several scripts, with marks, apostrophes and hyphens.
- **The country becomes a list.** Its free text reaches an AI that writes for the Manager. The guests that are stored keep their text (decision 8), and task 2 reads the data first, so that the list stores what the hotel's data already uses.
- **Nothing is cleaned.** The plan forbids stripping or rewriting a value: encoding at the output and parameters in the query stay the defence against markup and query text, as the OWASP cheat sheets say.
- **What is sound is said.** The read of the code found no query built from text and no guest text in markup; the milestone adds rules and tests and is not a rebuild.
- **Mode.** Autonomous, as in RC-410.
