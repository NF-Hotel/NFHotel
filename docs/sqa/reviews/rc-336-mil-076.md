## Findings

- **The clear dump never reaches the disk.** The read-back reads the stream between `pg_dump` and `age` in pipes inside the postgres container; the VM still cannot decrypt a backup (ADR-0008), and the test of criterion 1 looks for a clear file.
- **What the read-back proves, and what it does not.** It proves that the archive is whole and that `pg_restore` can read every block and list every table. It does not prove that a restore gives the same rows; the monthly drill keeps proving that, with the key of S03.
- **The new check stops no deploy.** Every deploy and every migration runs the backup first and stops when it fails (`deploy-release.sh`), so a failed read-back only reports and alerts until S01 decides at the gate, after seven nights.
- **The time of the backup.** The two readers run beside the dump, not after it; criterion 3 bounds the extra time at 5 minutes.
- **Not independent.** The AI that walked the checklists wrote the plan; the acceptance is S01's decision.
