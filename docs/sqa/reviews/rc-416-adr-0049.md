## Findings

- **F1, fixed: where the factor of four comes from.** The draft said that [ADR-0015] chose the factor for the address
  of the mobile sign-in. ADR-0015 says that the throttle also counts per client address; the factor of four is in the
  code that carries it out (`AddressLoginThrottle.Multiplier`). The text now says that the limit per address of the
  staff sign-in uses that factor since ADR-0015.
- **F2, fixed: 122 bits against ASVS 11.5.1.** The draft quoted 11.5.1 ("UUIDs do not respect this condition") without
  saying, in the decision, why that is no objection. Decision 2 now says it: the reference has 122 random bits, is not
  used as a value that must not be guessed, and the pair of reference and email behind the limits of decision 6 opens
  a booking. The verdict of 11.5.1 for the whole flow is given in SAR-002, task 1 of MIL-089.
- **F3, note: the answer holds no payment text.** Decision 7 of [MIL-085] allows the payment text in the answer; the
  ADR leaves it to the page, which writes it today (`PaymentText`). One field fewer than allowed meets ASVS 15.3.1
  better and changes no page. Accepted by the AI as stand-in for S01.
- **F4, note: decision 8 removes the older endpoint.** [RC-388] F4 left to S01 whether `POST api/bookings/public/receipt`
  is removed (action item, due 2026-12-31). [MIL-085] decision 8 asks the ADR to settle it, and the ADR removes it:
  one door is kept equal more easily than two, and no page calls it. The AI decides this as stand-in for S01, at S01's
  word in chat ("Du skal tage beslutningerne for mig"); the action item of RC-388 is closed by the pull request of
  MIL-085.
- **F5, note: the address a caller can write.** Until [MIL-087], the limit per address counts the first entry of
  `X-Forwarded-For`, which a caller can set. The ADR names it as a negative, keeps the address behind one port so that
  MIL-087 changes one implementation, and the limit per reference holds regardless. Accepted as stand-in for S01 for
  the days between the two milestones.
- **F6, note: the guest page shows less.** Decision 7 drops the name and the email of the booking from the guest page.
  [MIL-085] decision 7 lists what the answers hold and not the name; the page loses a section that repeated what the
  guest typed. Accepted as stand-in for S01; [UC-010] follows with the documents of task 5.

## Overall Verdict

Go. Every Mandatory criterion of QC-ADR-001 passes after the fixes of F1 and F2, and the ADR settles what task 1 of
[MIL-085] asks of it: the alternatives with the reason each lost, the limit per caller address with its number (20 in
15 minutes) and its bounded store (10,000 windows), and the older lookup endpoint (removed). The AI accepted ADR-0049
and this record as stand-in for S01 in autonomous mode, at S01's word in chat on 2026-11-19; the row of ADR-0049 is
Accepted. The reviewer is not independent: an AI wrote the ADR and this record. S01 may challenge any decision of it,
F3 to F6 above all.
