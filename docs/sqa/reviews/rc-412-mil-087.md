## Findings

- **An address is not a person.** Several guests share one address behind a carrier or an office. The numbers of decision 2 leave room for that, and the refusal says when to try again.
- **The count per email tells a little, until MIL-088.** A refusal for an email with three coming bookings tells a prober that the email has them. It has the status and the text of the other limits; once a booking is verified by email, only the owner of the mailbox meets it.
- **Counts in memory are lost at a restart.** That is accepted and written down in decision 5: every deploy starts the windows again.
- **The check on the live site makes no booking.** Criterion 8 tests the limit of the searches there, which stores nothing; the limit on booking requests is shown on the throwaway stack with the numbers of production.
- **The numbers of the two alerts are a first guess for a small hotel.** ADR-0051 may change them after a week of counters.
- **Not here: a puzzle for humans and a rule at Cloudflare.** Both need an action of S01 in an account; the decision record names them as the next step.
- **The address in the audit trail could be written by the caller.** That is a defect of today, found while reading; decision 4 closes it for the audit trail and for the limits at once.
- **Mode.** Autonomous, as in RC-410.
