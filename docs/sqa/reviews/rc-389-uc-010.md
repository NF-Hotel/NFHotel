## Findings

- **F1, fixed: an actor and a goal that the diagram and the stories did not have.** UC-010 said in its open issues
  that [UCD-001], [US-001] and [SA-001] had no guest, which fails criterion 10. The guest is now S06 of [SA-001], the
  actor Guest with the use case View and Change Own Booking in [UCD-001], and US-001.13 in [US-001]; the stakeholder
  S06 is in UC-010, and the two open issues on the diagram and on UC-004 are removed (reviewed in [RC-390]).
- **F2, fixed: the parameters of changeDates.** The message of [SSD-001] said `changeDates(arrivalDate,
  departureDate)` and the contract `changeDates(startDate: DateOnly, endDate: DateOnly)`, which fails criterion 1 of
  QC-OC-001. The message now uses the names of the contract, the IT terms of [DICT-001], and the operations table
  gives the PO terms beside them.
- **F3, note: permission names in a business rule.** The rule for "all" steps names `Bookings.Create`,
  `Bookings.EditOwn` and `Guests.ViewOwn`. They are the rows of the Access Control Matrix, which is the business
  table of who may do what, and UC-004 cites it the same way, so the use case stays free of user interface and code.
- **F4, note: the 30 days.** The open issue that every cancelled booking is refused, not only one cancelled more than
  30 days ago, stays in UC-010 as [ADR-0047] decision 7 and [RC-388] F3 say.
