## Findings

Each finding as the second AI gave it, with what the reworked version of [ADR-0053] does about it; [RC-434] reviews
the reworked version.

- **3, the fuse part.** The mails were counted before the send and recorded after it, so a burst of requests could pass
  the fuse. Reworked: decision 6 counts under the lock of [ADR-0052] decision 6, with the record written before the
  mail is sent.
- **5. Two ways to stop the booking.** A sender with 20 addresses, 20 requests each and 80 emails uses up the fuse and
  stops every public booking for up to a day, every day; and Google stops an account from sending for a day after
  many mails that bounce, which invalid addresses cause sooner and which stops S01's private mail too. Reworked: both
  are negative consequences with the response (the alert `MailVolumeHigh` first, then a rule of Cloudflare or the
  puzzle of [ADR-0051], or fewer requests per address; an account made for the hotel).
- **6. The private mailbox.** Gmail keeps every mail sent through it in Sent, and bounces land in the inbox, so S01's
  private mailbox would collect the email of every guest, the dates, a working link and the reference, which with the
  email opens a booking (`GuestAccessService` at 6f7bb80, [ADR-0047]); that is out of reach of the clean-up. The second AI asked
  that S01 agrees in chat before the acceptance. Reworked: a negative consequence, the wiki page recommends an account
  made for the hotel, and decision 8 makes the choice of the account S01's before the first step of task 9 of
  [MIL-088], when the first mail of a guest could flow.
- **10. The mode of the secret files.** The draft said 0600; the secret files are 0444 inside a folder of 0700
  (`write-secret-files.sh`, [ADR-0024] decision 4). Reworked.
- **11. The scan of the catcher.** The daily scan of [MIL-064] skips only `proxmox` and reads every
  `ops/deploy/*/docker-compose.yml`, so a catcher in an override file of a stack would not be scanned. Reworked: the
  catcher has its own folder, `ops/deploy/mailpit`, which the scan reads.
- **12. The forwarder.** `nfhotel-api` also carries nginx and the web application, so a forwarder on it would be
  reachable from them; the addresses of Gmail change, so HAProxy needs a `resolvers` section as the database relay of
  [ADR-0034] has; and a separate setting of the forwarder could disagree with the settings of the API, with a missing
  file passing the start. Reworked: the networks `nfhotel-mail` (the API and the forwarder only) and
  `nfhotel-mail-out` (the forwarder only); the name server of Docker every 10 seconds; the host and the port taken from
  the settings of the API, with no file of the forwarder, and a deploy that refuses the settings that do not fit.
- **13. The claims of ASVS.** 12.3.1 covers every connection of the application, while [ADR-0006] and [ADR-0023]
  leave plain HTTP inside the virtual machine; the check of the certificate is 12.3.2 and the pin 12.3.4; 13.3.1 asks
  for a secrets-management solution, which the secret files are not. Reworked: each claim is scoped to the way of the
  mail, and 13.3.1 is a negative consequence that names the vault of [ADR-0024] as the next step.
- **14. Other facts.** MailKit 5.0.0 brings MimeKit.Core and System.Formats.Asn1, not one package; a bad value of
  `Smtp:Security` stopped every start in one place and only with the verification on in another. Reworked: both
  dependencies named, and one rule (a bad value stops every start).
- **15. The checklist.** Criteria 6, 7 and 10 above. Reworked: the quality attributes use the names of 2023; the wiki
  pages, Network-Isolation and the alert rules are named with the task that changes them, and every listed artifact is
  a link and in the CrossReference; TLS, SSL, STARTTLS, TCP and OAuth are spelled out on first use.

## Overall Verdict

Go-with-conditions. The design holds: SMTP over TLS through a port and an adapter, with a forwarder as the only way
out. The conditions were findings 3 (the fuse part), 5, 6 (S01 agrees in chat) and 10 to 15; criterion 7, which is
mandatory, fails until 15 is fixed. As stand-in for S01, the AI accepts that condition 6 is met by stating the risks
and leaving the choice of the account to S01 before task 9 of [MIL-088], because no mail of a guest flows before
then; the AI asks S01 the question through the planning session. The reviewer is not independent: an AI wrote the
ADR and this record, and a second AI gave the findings.
