## Findings

- **Nine images keep one finding that no release fixes yet.** `golang.org/x/net` older than 0.62.0 (CVE-2026-78661) is compiled into nine of the images, and no upstream release has 0.62.0 yet. The plan moves every pin that a newer release improves and accepts what is left for at most 30 days, instead of rebuilding upstream binaries. Criterion 1 is therefore met with accepted findings, and the Traceability row says so.
- **The ignore file is shared with the release images.** An entry without `paths` or `purls` would also hide the finding in the images that NF Hotel builds. The plan narrows every entry to the files or packages of a third-party image, which a test with Trivy 0.75.0 showed that it honours, and criterion 5 checks the file.
- **The accepted findings need S01.** Section 8 of DSA-001 names S01 as the approver of an ignore entry, and a stand-in may not approve a risk exception. The entries can be prepared, but they wait for the approval of S01 in the review of the pull request (action item 3).
- **Two new builds are younger than the cooldown.** The new builds of `postgres:16-alpine` (2026-11-05) and `python:3.13-slim` (2026-11-08) are inside the seven days of `.github/dependabot.yml`. They are rebuilds of the pinned tags with the security fixes of their distributions, and the cooldown keeps out a new release that could be compromised, so the plan takes them. Prometheus stays at v3.15.1, the version past the cooldown, because v3.16.0 leaves the same finding.
- **The VM must not build.** Once the socket proxy is a release image, a failed pull of its digest would make compose build the image on the VM. Task 4 has the deploy of a release pass `--no-build` to compose, so the deploy stops instead (ADR-0007).

## Overall Verdict

Go-with-conditions. Every Mandatory criterion of QC-MIL-001 passes. The stand-in does not accept the plan: MIL-065 stays Proposed, and none of its changes is committed, until S01 accepts it in chat.
