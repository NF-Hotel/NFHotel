## Findings

- **The order is the point of the plan.** Task 1 closes findings 1 and 2 at the tunnel for every spelling: `cloudflared` validates the token before a request reaches the VM, while nginx only checks that the header is there. So task 1 comes first, and nothing of the milestone is pushed to the public repository before it has passed.
- **The fixes were checked before the plan.** The closed lab of 20:18 UTC, with the rules in the planned order: `cloudflared tunnel ingress validate` accepts them; `/admin/login`, `/ADMIN/login`, `/Admin/login`, `/aDmin/login`, `/admin` and `/administrator` take the validating route, and `/book`, `/mobile-api/api/rooms` and `/` the route without a path. With `^/admin` first, `/ADMIN/login` still took the route without a path (the first lab, 13:12 UTC). nginx with `location ~* ^/admin` gave 404 to ten spellings without the header and let `/admin/login` and `/ADMIN/login` through with it.
- **The plan matches the code of `main` at 1c2726b.** `location ^~ /admin` with the header check is in `ops/deploy/nginx/templates/public.conf.template`; the checks of the back office in `ops/tests/test-nginx.sh` use lower case only; Ops CI runs `test-nginx.sh` (`.github/workflows/ops-ci.yaml`); the image of `cloudflared` in `ops/deploy/cloudflared/docker-compose.yml` is 2026.10.7 with the digest of the lab; decision 2 of ADR-0029 names the path `^/admin` and no order; the wiki page Public-Access, step 4 of the Access setup, adds the route without saying where it goes in the list.
- **Production is touched only as the plan says.** The acceptance is S01's yes to the GET requests of tasks 1 and 5, without an Access session and without a sign-in attempt, and S01's answer "2.dig" is the yes to the change of the routes by the AI in S01's browser. Nothing else in production changes under this record.
- **The registry's next RC number was stale.** It said 325 although RC-325 to RC-329 exist (MIL-071); it was set to 330 before this record.
- **The acceptance covers the language and the terms.** As for MIL-070 ([RC-318]), criteria 3, 5 and 9 rest on the acceptance of S01.
